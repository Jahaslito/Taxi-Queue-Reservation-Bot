FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does SanQueue do?
SanQueue is queue-management software that automates a participant's position in a queue — securing their place, timing entry precisely, and surfacing live status — for any operation where people or vehicles wait in line.
What kinds of operations is it for?
Anywhere there's a line: airport ground-transport dispatch, seaports and cruise terminals, depots and dispatch yards, logistics and freight check-in, events and venues, and appointment-based service lines.
How accurate is the positioning?
Our pre-armed session technology fires at the exact second, landing within a tight, predictable position window. We continuously validate timing against real operational logs to keep it precise.
Is it secure?
Yes. Credentials are encrypted at rest, never stored in plain text, and used solely to perform the queueing action you authorize. Security and trust are built into the platform.
Do you have a live product today?
Yes — SanQueue for airport drivers is live at San Diego International Airport, automating eDispatch queue placement every day. See the products page for the full details.
How do we get started?
Drivers can start a 14-day free trial today. Operators, ports, and enterprises can book a demo — call (858) 201-3255 and we'll design a queue program for your operation.
