Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 10, 2026 · SAN Queue LLC

SAN Queue LLC ("SAN Queue", "we", "us") provides an automated queue scheduler and dispatch alert service for taxi drivers operating at San Diego International Airport. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why, and how we handle it.

1. Information we collect

When you sign up and use the service, we collect:

  • Account details — your name, email, mobile phone number, and a password you choose for our app.
  • Airport credentials — your SAN eDispatch username, password, and vehicle (cab) number. Your SAN password is encrypted at rest using AES-256.
  • Schedule preferences — the time- or position-based settings you configure in the app.
  • Operational logs — records of when our automation queued or removed your vehicle, the resulting queue position, and timestamps. We use these to monitor reliability and to show you your own activity history.
  • Billing information — handled by our payment processor Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • Location data — only when you fire an SOS alert. See section 3 below.

2. SMS dispatch alerts

By signing up and providing your mobile phone number, you consent to receive transactional SMS dispatch alerts from SAN Queue. These messages tell you when San Diego Airport has dispatched your vehicle to Terminal 1 or Terminal 2, along with your name, cab number, and the 25-minute window to reach the terminal.
  • Frequency: only when you are dispatched (typically 1–6 times per day; you control how often by how much you drive).
  • We never use your number for marketing, sales, or promotional messages.
  • You can stop SMS at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for support contact.
  • Standard message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier.
  • SMS delivery is handled by our messaging provider Telnyx. Your phone number is shared with Telnyx solely to deliver these messages.

3. SOS alerts and location sharing

When you fire an SOS alert from the SAN Queue app, your live device location is shared with the SAN Queue admin team so they can dispatch help.
  • The SOS feature is opt-in per event — your location is only accessed at the moment you press and hold the SOS button.
  • If you enable "Live Tracking" during an active SOS, your location continues to update until you cancel the alert or admin resolves it.
  • Outside an active SOS alert, the app does not read or transmit your location. We do not run background location tracking.
  • Location data attached to an SOS alert is retained alongside the alert record for incident review and may be shared with emergency services if needed.

4. Push notifications

If you enable browser push notifications in the app, your device registers a push endpoint with our server. We use this only to deliver the same dispatch alerts that go out by SMS. You can disable push notifications from your device settings or by tapping the in-app toggle at any time.

5. How we use your information

  • To operate the queue automation on your behalf at SAN eDispatch.
  • To send you transactional dispatch SMS and push notifications.
  • To handle SOS alerts and contact you about an emergency.
  • To process your subscription billing through Stripe.
  • To debug and improve the service.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

6. Service providers we share data with

  • Telnyx — outbound SMS delivery (your phone number + message body).
  • Stripe — subscription billing (your email + payment details you provide to Stripe directly).
  • Resend — transactional emails such as password resets and email verification.
  • Web push services (Apple, Google, Mozilla) — delivering push notifications to your device.

7. Data retention

We keep your account information and operational logs for as long as your account is active. SOS alerts and the location data attached to them are retained for incident review. You may delete your account at any time by contacting us — we will remove your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., billing records).

8. Your rights

  • Opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP.
  • Disable push notifications from your device or in the app.
  • Request a copy or deletion of your data by emailing us.
  • Withdraw consent for SMS or location processing at any time.

9. Security

We encrypt sensitive credentials at rest, use HTTPS for all data in transit, and restrict admin access to the operational team. No system is perfectly secure, but we treat your data as if it were our own.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified via the app or by email before they take effect.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email info@sanqueue.com.
