FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it safe to give SanQueue my SAN eDispatch login?
Yes. Your SAN password is encrypted with AES-256 before it ever touches our database, and your SanQueue account password is hashed with bcrypt. We can never see it in plain text. Credentials are used for one purpose only: logging you into eDispatch to secure your queue spot.
How does the automatic queueing actually work?
At the time you choose, SanQueue securely logs into the SAN eDispatch portal, finds your vehicle, and adds you to the queue, then records your position and notifies you. You don't have to open anything or be awake.
What if I want to change my time or skip a day?
You're always in control. Set a different time for each day of the week, switch between time-based and position-based scheduling, or pause whenever you like; all from your phone in seconds.
How accurate is my queue position?
SanQueue uses pre-armed sessions that fire at the exact second, so you typically land within ±10 positions of where you aimed. We continuously validate timing against real dispatch logs to keep it tight.
What does it cost, and is there a trial?
SanQueue Driver is $16/month with a 14-day free trial. Cancel anytime, no contracts. Operators and ports get custom pricing; just call us.
Do you support queues outside the airport?
That's where we're headed. SanQueue is a queue-management platform: seaports, cruise terminals, depots and other queue-based operations are on our roadmap. If you run one, call (858) 201-3255 and let's talk.
